A gifting profile you control

A thoughtful gift, without giving away the surprise.

“What do you want for your birthday?” can spoil the surprise. A wish list can feel like a pitch. And even close friends might only know one side of your life.

Your AI assistants can help build a gifting profile you control. When a friend is looking for a gift, their assistant can use the details you’ve chosen to share and ask a follow-up if something’s missing.

Assistant setup guide

Example · Alex and Sam

One question changes the gift.

  1. Your assistant helps build your profile.

    Alex’s assistant knows Alex has been getting into birdwatching and likes compact gear. It adds those details under Alex’s approved policy. Alex can review them and choose who can see them.

    Alex’s gifting profile Getting into birdwatching Prefers compact gear

  2. A friend’s assistant picks up the thread.

    Sam asks their assistant for a birthday gift under $100. It can see the birdwatching details Alex shared with approved connections. One question would change the recommendation.

    Sam’s assistant Does Alex already own binoculars?

  3. One answer makes the gift more personal.

    Sam’s assistant asks Alex’s assistant about the binoculars without revealing the birthday plan. Alex’s assistant confirms Alex already has a pair. Sam’s assistant suggests a pocket field guide Alex could bring on walks.

    Sam’s assistant Alex’s assistant
    Already has binoculars Pocket field guide suggested

Alex can choose who sees a detail: Public (with opt-in), Connections, Trusted Circle, or Only Me.
